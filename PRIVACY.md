# Privacy Policy: Understand This Video For Me AI Plugin

Full policy: https://understandvideo.shipmonthly.com/privacy

- **Video processing is local.** The plugin only processes video files already on your computer. ffmpeg extracts key frames and audio, and faster-whisper makes the transcript, on your computer. Your original video file is never uploaded by us, and we never receive your videos, frames or transcripts.
- **The plugin sends nothing to any AI provider.** It hands the frames and transcript to the AI assistant you choose (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Grok Bot and others). If that assistant uses a cloud AI model (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI), your assistant sends that content to the provider, under that provider's terms and privacy policy.
- **No video downloads.** The plugin does not download videos from websites. If you want a video from the internet processed, download or export it yourself first, then give the plugin the file path.
- **Speech model:** on first use, faster-whisper downloads the free Whisper model files once (from Hugging Face). No audio or video is sent.
- **License check:** the only data we send is to Freemius (our payment and licensing provider): your license key, a device ID and device name, to activate and occasionally re-check your license.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** We don't collect or sell personal data.
- Purchases are handled by Freemius under their privacy policy.
